# Welcome to Your Second Brain

This is your **Inbox** - the starting point for all new ideas, notes, and captures.

## How to Use This Vault

### The PARA Method

Your vault is organized using the PARA method:

| Folder | Purpose | Example |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **0-Inbox** | Capture everything here first | Quick notes, ideas, links |
| **1-Projects** | Active projects with deadlines | "Launch website", "Write report" |
| **2-Areas** | Ongoing areas of responsibility | Health, Finance, Career |
| **3-Resources** | Reference material & interests | Book notes, tutorials, recipes |
| **4-Archive** | Completed or inactive items | Past projects, old references |

### Workflow

1. **Capture** - Drop everything into `0-Inbox/`
2. **Clarify** - Process inbox items: what is it? Is it actionable?
3. **Organize** - Move to the right PARA folder
4. **Review** - Weekly review to keep things current
5. **Archive** - Move completed projects to `4-Archive/`
